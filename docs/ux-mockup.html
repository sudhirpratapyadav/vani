vani · UX redesign · recommended direction

One instrument, eight states

Every session surface — tray prose, notification banners, the caption box — collapses into a single morphing pill. State is carried by color and motion (readable from peripheral vision), words appear only when you choose to look, and each transition has a sound. Live renderings below; the dark strips stand in for your desktop.

Option C — the native end-state

On GNOME Wayland the architecturally correct overlay is a Shell extension: an indicator in the top bar (where GNOME already puts mic-privacy dots) plus a shell-layer HUD that cannot steal focus. Same daemon, same event vocabulary, different renderer.

The earcon vocabulary

Four sounds, total. Quiet, distinct by contour, all under 150 ms, disableable with ui.sounds = false. Sound is the whole interface when your eyes are elsewhere — and the fallback when the pill is hidden.
